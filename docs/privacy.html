Cashflow Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Cashflow is a record book for money that comes in and goes out. It does not move money, connect to a bank, or hold an account for you. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

Your records and the text of the alerts you capture stay on your device. This version of Cashflow does not send them anywhere, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app handles

  • Records you enter: amount, currency, direction (in or out), date, name, account label, note and similar fields. They are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Payment alerts (optional): if you turn on Capture, Cashflow uses Android's notification access to read notifications, but only from the apps you choose under "Apps to watch". The text is read on your device to create a record or a Review item. Notifications from other apps are ignored. You can pause Capture or turn off notification access in Android settings at any time.
  • Text you paste: if you paste an alert into the app, it is processed the same way, on your device.
  • List of installed apps: to let you choose which apps to watch, the app reads the names and icons of apps installed on your device. This list is not stored or sent anywhere.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to export. If you tap Export CSV, the app creates a CSV file of your records and opens Android's share sheet so you can send it where you decide. Cashflow does not see where you send it.

This version has no internet permission, so it cannot transmit your data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. There are no advertising or analytics services in the app.

Storage and deletion

Data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Android backup is turned off for the app. You can delete a record inside the app (and restore it from Recovery). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data.

Children

Cashflow is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Future changes

We may later add optional features, such as AI help that analyses alerts or a paid subscription. If a feature needs to send alert text or other data off your device, it will be off by default, it will ask you first, and this policy and the Google Play data-safety information will be updated before it ships.
