Research behind this proposal — borrowed vs rejected (collapsible)
Copilot Money
  • Borrow: trusted category glyphs, tappable rows with detail editing, quick add always one thumb away, a dedicated “to review” surface.
  • Reject: budgets or Free-to-Spend graphs, “all new transactions must be reviewed”, bank-balance framing.
  • Their Review demands attention on every entry; ours is only for genuinely ambiguous or unparseable alerts.
Sources: copilot.money · Transactions tab · Dashboard tab
Things 3
  • Borrow: optional details tucked behind one calm expander; one identical plus action everywhere; clear date headings; short purposeful motion.
  • Reject: any project/list organisation metaphor — payments are one stream, not projects.
Source: culturedcode.com/things/features
Cash App
  • Borrow: bold personality, compact number-first hero and keypad that make the main action physical and fast.
  • Reject: the implication of sending money — Cashflow never initiates or reverses payments; save means “write in the book”.
Source: cash.app
Buddy
  • Borrow: the friendly register of an everyday money app — warm language, one accent that smiles.
  • Reject: dashboard/budget modules and custom category colour themes — totals stay one honest set.
Source: buddy.download
